## 🔒 What Is keyboard-lock?

keyboard-lock is a simple, safe utility that temporarily disables your keyboard so you can wipe it clean without accidentally pressing keys, launching apps, or typing nonsense. It's perfect for cleaning your desk, keyboard, or laptop before, during, or after those messy snack sessions.

This app runs quietly in the background and works on Windows, macOS, and Linux. It's designed to be dark, slim, and unobtrusive—just like your favorite night-mode apps.

## 🖥️ How to Use keyboard-lock

## 💡 Troubleshooting & FAQs

### "Windows says this app is unrecognized"
Click **More info** → **Run anyway**. This happens with new software. The app is safe and open-source.

### "The keyboard still works after pressing Lock"
Make sure the app window is active (click it once) before pressing Lock. Also check that your keyboard is USB or built-in; some exotic Bluetooth keyboards may need a reconnect.

### "Can I lock only part of the keyboard?"
No, keyboard-lock locks all keys at once. This keeps things simple and safe.

### "I'm stuck—how do I get my keyboard back?"
Use your mouse to click **Cancel** in the app window, or press `Esc` on your keyboard. If all else fails, restart your computer.

### "Where is my download file?"
Most browsers save to the **Downloads** folder. Press `Ctrl+J` in your browser to see your downloads.

## 🧰 Advanced Users & Developers

keyboard-lock is built with **TypeScript**, **Electron**, and a focus on cross-platform reliability. It respects your privacy—no telemetry, no accounts, no data collection.

For advanced configuration, you can create a file named `settings.json` in the same folder as the app with options like:

```json
{
  "defaultLockTime": 120,
  "cancelKey": "Escape",
  "minimizeToTray": true
}
```

Changes take effect next time you open the app.
